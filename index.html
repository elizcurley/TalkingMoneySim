Nonnegotiable practice rules

Be compelling.
Be accountable.

Don't invent outcomes. Delivery counts, anecdotes, and intentions are not proof that people's lives improved.

Don't erase collaborators. Credit staff, community partners, participants, and the limits of your own contribution.

Don't turn people into props. Protect dignity, consent, confidentiality, self-determination, and community voice.

Don't promise a program you can't operate. Staff time, funding, supervision, data burdens, and sustainability matter.
